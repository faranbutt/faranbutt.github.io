Faran Taimoor Butt
MS Student · Applied Mathematics & Computer Science · MIPT Moscow

I am an MS student at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT), where my thesis — GuadPEFT — investigates socially responsible parameter-efficient fine-tuning of large language models, targeting bias amplification and alignment failures during adapter-based adaptation.

Simultaneously, I am a Research Mentee at BASIRA Lab, Imperial College London (I-X) under Prof. Islem Rekik, building GraphFMD — a graph-based benchmark for financial fraud detection targeting NeurIPS. I previously interned at the National Center of Artificial Intelligence (NCAI), contributing a sensor-fusion SLAM system for an autonomous wheelchair.

My research spans machine learning, graph neural networks, computer vision, NLP, robotics, and AI safety. I am actively seeking PhD opportunities for Fall 2026 and welcome collaborations.
