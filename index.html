01 — About

Building AI that is
safe and trustworthy

I am an MS student in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science at the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT), where my thesis explores GuadPEFT — parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods designed to reduce bias amplification and alignment risks in large language models.

I am a Research Mentee at BASIRA Lab, Imperial College London (I-X Initiative), working on graph neural networks and explainable AI under Prof. Islem Rekik. My ongoing work, GraphFMD, is a graph-based financial fraud detection benchmark targeting NeurIPS.

My broader goal is to contribute to the foundations of AI Safety and Alignment — understanding when and why language models fail, and building systems that behave reliably, honestly, and robustly in the real world. I am actively seeking PhD opportunities for Fall 2026.
